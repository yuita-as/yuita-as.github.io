Professional profile

Yuita Arum Sari, Ph.D. is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Informatics, Faculty of Computer Science, Universitas Brawijaya. Her work connects artificial intelligence, computer vision, and health informatics with practical human needs — from food and nutrition assessment to biomedical imaging, mental-health signals, and assistive systems.

She leads the Vision-Language Systems research group, built so that every undergraduate student has a pathway from learner to researcher, from research to publication, and from innovation to real-world impact.
