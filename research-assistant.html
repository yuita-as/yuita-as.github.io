Research assistant

The Vision-Language Systems group gives undergraduate students a pathway from learner to researcher — working on real projects, co-authoring papers, and presenting at conferences.

Apply by emailSee current projects

What research assistants do

  • Collect, clean, and annotate image, speech, and text datasets
  • Build and evaluate computer-vision and deep-learning models
  • Turn experiments into undergraduate theses and co-authored papers
  • Present results at national and international conferences
  • Join competitions with research-based innovations

How to apply

  1. Choose a topic that interests you.
  2. Email yuita@ub.ac.id with your CV, transcript, and a short note on why that topic.
  3. Meet for a short discussion and a starter task.
