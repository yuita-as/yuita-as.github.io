About

Full bio →

Assistant Professor in the Department of Informatics, Faculty of Computer Science, Universitas Brawijaya, and a permanent faculty member since 2016. My work brings computer vision, artificial intelligence, and health informatics together for food and nutrition assessment, biomedical imaging, mental-health signals, and assistive systems.

Ph.D. from Okayama University (MEXT scholar) and Master Mobility at Warsaw University of Technology (Erasmus Mundus). I also lead FILKOM’s Integrated Counseling Service, serve on the Faculty Academic Senate as a DTIF representative, and am a FILKOM Change Agent for inclusive learning.

“AI is most valuable when it becomes understandable, useful, and close to the people it is designed to support.”
